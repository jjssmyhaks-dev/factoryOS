# ADR-009: Document extraction — multimodal LLM + validators, OCR fallback

- **Status**: accepted (embedding/OCR vendor **[VERIFY]**)
- **Date**: 2026-09-21
- **Deciders**: founder (SRB), PRD §4.4
- **Context**: Indian supplier invoices arrive as photos (blur, skew, Hindi/English mix, handwritten notes); classic OCR pipelines underperform on layout variety while multimodal LLMs are costlier per page.
- **Decision**: **Multimodal LLM with structured output (zod-validated) plus deterministic validators (§E8-S3); managed OCR (e.g. Textract) as fallback** when the model's confidence is low or cost targets are missed.
- **Alternatives considered**: OCR-first + LLM post-process (rejected for Phase 1: weaker on messy photos; revisit with data); pure OCR (rejected: no reasoning over partial fields).
- **Consequences**: cost per document metered from day one (NFR-9 ≤ ₹3); validators are the safety net, not the model.
- **Verification**: extraction accuracy on the golden set; region availability and quality of OCR on Indian documents **[VERIFY]** (E0/E8 spike).
